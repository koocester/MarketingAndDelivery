# Discovery Call — Extraction Question Set (Content Strategist)

> The Content Strategist runs this on a call with the guest or client before a shoot. The goal is to extract everything needed to build the storyboard: the offer, the lead magnet, the hooks, the arcs, the visuals, and the call to action. Ask one at a time. Listen more than you talk. Coach the guest to answer topic-first and in stories, not stats.

## How to run it

- **One ask per question. Keep each under ten words where you can.** Split tangled questions.
- **Pull authority through a lesson, insight, or story, never a raw stat or self-praise.** When they drop a number, ask the follow-up that turns it into a story ("what changed when you crossed that?").
- **Chase the burning follow-up.** When they say something valuable, go off script: ask what the viewer would shout at the screen.
- **Record or transcribe the call.** The transcript is what the storyboard is built from.
- Paid video: do this together with the interviewee; you may show the storyboard draft or output. Unpaid video: do it yourself while discussing, without showing the storyboard. In both cases, never show the GUIDE SOP itself — it is confidential, and only the storyboard output is shareable.

---

## 1. Context and logistics

1. Is this a paid or unpaid video?
2. Which page is it for? (Homes / Business / Autos / Foodie / other)
3. Where will we shoot, and what does the space look like?
4. What can we film there that most people never get to see?

## 2. The person and the proof

5. Who are you, and what do you do?
6. What did you build, and how big is it now?
7. What did that look like when you started?
8. What is the smartest thing you did in this business?
9. What almost broke it, and what changed?
10. What do you know now that you wish you knew then?

## 3. The offer and the lead magnet (design to Hormozi standard)

11. What do you sell, and who buys it?
12. What does the buyer's life look like after they get it? (the dream outcome)
13. Why is it stupid for the right person to say no?
14. What would make this offer feel irresistible for this video?
15. What can we give the viewer for free that they would actually want? (the lead magnet)
16. What guarantee or risk reversal can you stand behind?

> Paid video: the offer is by the client. Unpaid video: the offer is by us, and the call to action drives our lead generation.

## 4. The audience and their pain

17. Who is this video for, specifically?
18. What are they struggling with right now?
19. What do they already believe that is wrong?
20. What objection stops them from acting?
21. What do you want them to do after watching?

## 5. What winning looks like

22. What should the viewer walk away with? (information, emotion, inspiration, status)
23. What does a win from this video look like for you? (set it, do not overpromise)
24. What does a win look like for the business? (fulfilment and word of mouth, or leads)

## 6. Story material for the arcs

25. Walk me through the tour: what here is genuinely worth the money?
26. What is the one thing here you are most proud of?
27. What did it take to get to this point that people do not see?
28. What is the mid-point moment where someone realises this is real?
29. What is the honest, "wish someone had told me" advice for your audience?
30. If someone remembers only one thing from this, what should it be?

## 7. Hook fuel

31. What is the most surprising number in your business?
32. What is the boldest true thing you could say about your field?
33. What would make someone stop scrolling in under two seconds here?
34. What is a belief in your industry you completely disagree with?

## 8. Visuals and the shoot

35. What products, spaces, or details should we make sure to show?
36. What is the most visual, most cinematic thing here?
37. Is there anything sensitive we must not film or must clear first?
38. What will you be wearing? (steer to the objective's colour cluster; avoid white, beige, grey, navy, black, pastels, earth tones, and the location's dominant colour)

## 9. The call to action

39. What single action do you want the viewer to take?
40. What can you genuinely deliver if a lot of people take it?
41. What is the shortest way to say that call to action?

---

## Before you leave the call

- Do you have three possible hooks, each strong enough to stop a scroll?
- Do you have a story for every arc, not just a fact?
- Do you know the offer, the lead magnet, and the exact call to action?
- Ask yourself: what would the viewer shout at the screen? Get that answer before you hang up.

> Output of this call: a full transcript plus your notes, ready to feed into Poppy AI and draft the storyboard into the M&D Video table.
