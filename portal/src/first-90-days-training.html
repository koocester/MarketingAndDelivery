Part one · the universal timeline

The path everyone walks

First 24 hours → Week 1 → Week 2 → 30 → 60 → 90. Same rhythm, every role.

First 24 hours · all five on day one

Day one is setup. Finish all five.

  1. Join every required Lark group — company, your department, and your role channel.
  2. Read your department Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) — how your team actually works.
  3. Meet your manager — introduce yourself, agree how you'll check in.
  4. Check your Weekly Priorities — what matters this week, in writing.
  5. Understand your Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) — the numbers you'll be measured on.
All five, day one. None of this is "later in the week" — you can't do the work until you're plugged in and you know the standard.

How we work · the standard, day one to forever
The rules · non-negotiable

These are THE five rules.

Learn them once. They apply on day one, at day 90, and every day after — this is how we work, always.

1
Every task carries an owner, a deadline, and what success looks like no orphan work — someone owns it, by when, and you both know what "done" means (the definition of done)
2
Meetings create action itemsif a meeting ends with no owned next step, it didn't count
3
No task = no workif it's not a task in the system, it isn't happening
4
Speed > perfectionship, then improve — polish in the open
5
Clarity > assumptionsask the question, get it right the first time
