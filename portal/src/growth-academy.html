How a class runs

The same four beats every time, whichever market you are teaching in.
Step one

Prepare

Pull the deck and the handout for your class below. Check the market notes for local examples and pricing.

Step two

Deliver

Teach to the standard, not to the room. Same structure, same core examples, localised only where the notes say so.

Step three

Capture

Collect attendance and contact details. Every class is also a lead source, and it only counts if it is logged.

Step four

Feed back

Tell us what landed and what did not. The curriculum improves from the room, not from a meeting.
