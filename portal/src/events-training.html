Part two

The events handover

How an event becomes content.

The key move · one button
An event becomes content when you hand it over.

On the event record, press "Need Marketing?". It notifies the Marketing Updates group, the Event Updates group, and Mike (Head of Growth) with: "This event needs marketing support. Please create a Project so the team can start promoting it, then link the Project on the event record."

Why it pings Mike, not the Page

The button's whole job is to get a Project created.

Most events have no Page yet when you click. So the button pings Mike directly — not via the Page — because there's nothing to route through yet. Getting a Project (and its Page) created in the first place is the point.

No Page, no problem. That's exactly why you press the button — to create the Project and Page that don't exist yet.

Part three

Prepare the event

A controlled path from approved idea to event day.

Streamlined flow · step by step

Eight locks before the doors open.

Brief + outcomeAudience, purpose, metric, target and owner.
Approval + budgetScope, spend ceiling and decision maker confirmed.
Date + venueCapacity, access, permits, insurance and backup option.
Partners + suppliersQuotes, deliverables, sponsors, AV, catering and transport.
Guests + registrationForm, CRM tracking, confirmations and reminder sequence.
Programme + rolesRun-of-show, host, speakers, team rota and escalation owner.
Marketing + capturePromo plan, video/carousel angles, consent and lead capture.
Final readinessWalkthrough, call sheet, contingency check and go/no-go.
Logistics arrangement · three workstreams

One owner. One live plan. No verbal-only tasks.

Venue + suppliers

  • Contract, access hours and capacity
  • AV, power, Wi-Fi, furniture and signage
  • Catering, transport and loading
  • Weather, safety and backup plan

Guests + front of house

  • Registration and attendee data
  • Confirmations, reminders and directions
  • Check-in, badges and guest flow
  • Accessibility, VIPs and consent

Programme + team

  • Run-of-show and call sheet
  • Hosts, speakers and cue owners
  • Content and lead-capture stations
  • Escalation path and close-down
Control rule: every task has an owner, deadline, status and proof of confirmation on the event record or linked plan.

Koocester Branded Events Checklist · criteria

It is Koocester-branded only when all six pass.

1 · Strategic fitOne defined audience, one useful promise and one measurable outcome.
2 · Brand fitPremium, useful and welcoming; approved identity, signage and tone.
3 · Lead engineRegistration, consent, source tracking and Sales handoff are built in.
4 · Content engineVideo and carousel angles, talent match and capture plan are decided early.
5 · ExperienceProgramme, guest flow, hosts, accessibility and service feel intentional.
6 · Commercial controlBudget, sponsors, suppliers, safety and contingency are approved and logged.
Go/no-go: if any criterion is unresolved, fix it or move the date. Branding is the full experience, not just the logo.
Koocester Branded Events Checklist · formats

Choose the format that serves the outcome.

Founder roundtableDepth, credibility and high-quality relationships.
Networking eventCommunity growth, introductions and lead volume.
Showcase or fairConsumer discovery, partners and on-site conversion.
Workshop or panelEducation, authority and a clear next action.
Community meetupAffinity-led experiences such as runs or car meets.
Format follows objective. Do not choose a gala, panel or fair because it looks impressive; choose it because it best moves the audience to the target.
Koocester Branded Events Checklist · do and don't

Protect the brand in the decisions.

Do

  • Set the objective, metric and target before spending.
  • Define the audience and one clear next action.
  • Suggest content angles, people and assets early.
  • Confirm every owner, supplier and contingency in writing.
  • Capture leads, consent, footage and actuals.
  • Debrief and carry the learning into the next event.

Don't

  • Run an event for attendance alone.
  • Mix audiences, promises or objectives.
  • Announce before venue, budget and owner are confirmed.
  • Improvise logos, signage or sponsor placement.
  • Depend on verbal coordination or an unowned task.
  • Close without Sales and Marketing handoffs.
Part four

The numbers

Metric, target, actual — on the record.

On the event's Project record

Pick the metric. Set the target. Log the actual.

Event Metric is a single-select — choose the one that matters for this event:

· Attendance pax · Sign-ups · Leads Collected · Revenue SGD · Other
Event Target (number) and Event Actual (number) sit alongside it. Outcome Progress auto-calculates Actual ÷ Target.
Read the gap, then act on it

Actual ÷ Target tells you the truth.

Attendance pax75%
Actual 150Target 200

150 of 200 seats filled — a 25% gap. Outcome Progress makes it a fact, not a feeling. Read the gap and act on it: what filled the seats you got, and what would have filled the rest?

Attendance is not the finish line
A full room is the start, not the win.

The seats are only worth what you do with them. Capture the leads in the room, log the actual, hand the leads to Sales, and follow up. An event with a full room and no captured leads didn't finish the job.

Run it clean · one slide

Venue, budget and sponsors — logged on the event.

Every event carries its budget, venue and sponsors on the record, so the numbers are honest and the room is handled. Sponsorship money flows to HubSpot / Xero — you keep the event side clean.

Cost per lead · the benchmark to beat

No CPL of your own yet — so benchmark it two ways.

Cost per lead = event spend ÷ leads captured. Until each event has its own history, judge it by market and by page — a lead is not one price.

By market

our blended cost per sign-up today
  • Singapore — priciest, ~S$39
  • Malaysia — mid, ~S$16
  • Indonesia — cheapest, ~S$7

By page

industry lead cost, high → low
  • Wealth & Homes — highest
  • Autos — mid
  • Food & lifestyle — lowest
Your job: log every event's spend and leads. Real per-event CPL replaces the benchmark fast — then you beat it.
