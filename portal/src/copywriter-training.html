Copywriter

The words that make content convert and travel.

Your mission

Write the words that carry it.

You find stories worth repurposing, then turn them into captions and carousels people act on and share. You work assigned projects and source strong outside reels yourself. You do not wait for finished videos.

What success looks like
You're winning when…
Slide one stops the scrollthe hook earns the tap, every time
Something in it travelsa save-worthy line, not just a click
The CTA is true to the sourceinherited from the video's offer, never invented
The head copywriter approves it cleanfirst pass, no rewrite needed
The monthly carousel mix is completeper Page: 12 standalone carousels = 4 Sensational + 8 Trust, one Sensational for every two Trust pieces

This is the bar. Everything that follows is how you hit it — the gate at the end checks you against it.

Where you sit

Two sources. One writing standard.

Assigned

  • Worthy video in the queue
  • Use its project brief and offer

Self-sourced

  • Outside reel or article you find
  • Record the source before writing
Chosen source→ Copywriting→ Head copywriter approval→ Social media manager

Ownership: Copywriters actively feed the pipeline. The queue is work to complete, not permission to stop sourcing.

Part one

In the base

Where you look, and when your clock starts.

Your home view

The Copywriter queue.

Work assigned carousels top to bottom, then keep sourcing from outside reels. The queue shows what is waiting; it is not the only place your next story can begin.

The clock
3h copywriting.

The clock starts only when you pick a carousel up and press Start copywriting. So the timer reflects real work, not time spent waiting in the queue.

Working-hours basis: Monday to Friday, 10:00–18:00 base time. Copywriting is 3h, amendments are 2h, and head-copywriter approval is 4h internal / 12h client.

How it reaches you

Two steps to your desk.

Pending Copywriting Waiting · no clock running Press
Start copywriting → Copywriting 3 hour clock running

The Send to Copywriter button drops a carousel into Pending Copywriting with no clock running. The clock starts only when you press Start copywriting to pick it up, and that starts the 3 hour copywriting clock. So the clock begins on pickup, not before.

Your two buttons
Start copywriting Submit carousel

Start copywriting opens your 3 hour clock, so press it before you write a word. Submit carousel sends your draft to the head copywriter.

What you fill
Carousel copy (Canva) Caption Source URL (outside reel or article)

The slides and the caption are yours. A bright-yellow cell means must fill. Never submit with one empty.

Outside reels and articles

Pull from the source.

Save the original URL, verify the story and pull the hook, facts and visual moments from the source. Outside reels are raw material, not copy to reproduce line for line.

Self-sourcing gate · before you build

A reel earns a carousel when it clears four checks.

Above-average viewsFor Koocester main, use 50k–100k views or higher as the signal.
Real valueA story, a wow factor or a reason the reader will care.
Conversion potentialThe topic can lead naturally to a high-converting call to action.
Prefer unpaid contentPaid client content may need to be removed later, especially when old.
The offer is inherited

Keep the call consistent.

Use the lead magnet already set on the project. For a self-sourced reel, get the Content Strategist or Marketing Coordinator to confirm the offer and automation keyword before you write the call to action. Never invent one alone.

Your first day
  1. Open the base and clear assigned carousels from the Copywriter queue.
  2. Source outside reels that clear the four checks; save the original URL.
  3. Read the source and project brief; confirm the offer and tone.
  4. Press Start copywriting, your 3 hour clock begins now, not before.
  5. Draft the copy and the caption, match the page's country and vertical.
  6. Submit for the head copywriter's approval.
  7. Check the Copywriter Updates channel daily.
Part two

The skill

Words that convert, words that travel.

Two jobs, every carousel

Make it convert

  • A hook on slide one
  • One clear payoff
  • The inherited call to action

Make it travel

  • Worth a save
  • Worth a send
  • A line they want to quote
Write it so it earns the tap
  • Slide one is the hook. If it does not stop the scroll, nothing else counts.
  • One idea per slide. Short lines. No wall of words.
  • End on the call the Source Video set.
  • Read it aloud. If it drags, cut it.
Video captions

Sharpen the draft.

An AI draft caption is generated from the transcript. Your job is to sharpen it before it posts. Fix the hook, cut the slack, make it sound like us.

Write for the page

Match where it lands.

Every piece sits on a Page, and the Page carries the vertical and the country. Write to that audience. A wealth reader in one market is not a food reader in another.

Part three

The gate

Who signs it off, and who reviews you.

Final approval

The Head Copywriter is the gate.

Ratnasari gives every carousel its final approval, company wide, before it can go to the Social Media Manager. Submit clean copy so it clears in one pass.

Who reviews you

The Head of Growth.

Your work is reviewed by the Head of Growth. Speed matters, but the standard is copy that converts and travels. Both are the measure.
