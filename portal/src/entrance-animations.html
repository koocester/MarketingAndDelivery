What works best for a player-select screen

The best character-select screens (Overwatch, Valorant, Fortnite, fighting games) almost always do three things: a staggered reveal (never all at once — it builds anticipation), a directional entrance where cards travel to their slots (it reads as "the roster assembling"), and a satisfying lock-in when you actually pick one.

From these, the strongest for player-select are 7 · Assemble (roster flying into place — the genre standard), 9 · Slam In (fighting-game impact, very premium), and 5 · Deal In (card-game energy). 8 · Spotlight is the most cinematic if you want it to feel like a reveal.

My recommendation: 7 · Assemble for the entrance — then I'd add a hover "focus" (card lifts + glows) and a "lock-in" flash on select, so the whole flow feels like picking a fighter.
