Part one

The hygiene rule

Three fields, never missing.

Your mission

No record you touch is missing the essentials.

Hygiene means Page, objective and assignee are always filled. A bright yellow cell means must fill, so clear it before you move on to the next record.

The hygiene rule
Page Objective Assignee

These three drive everything else. Miss one and the record can't be trusted downstream, not by the team, not by the dashboards.

Watch for orphans

No Page means no team.

A record with no Page is an orphan: no team gets assigned, so nobody works it. Hunt orphans down and clear duplicates before a deadline runs out on them.

How assignment works
Born at Not Started→ Page linked→ Assigned automatically

Records are born at Not Started and take their team from the Page. Your job is to catch the ones that slipped through without a Page, because those never got assigned.

Part two

Work the view

Top to bottom, until it's empty.

Where to look

The hygiene view.

It surfaces records missing key fields. Work it top to bottom until it is empty. An empty hygiene view is a base you can trust.

Why this matters

Dashboards are only as honest as the records feeding them.

A missing field doesn't just sit quietly, it breaks a rollup, skips an assignment, or shows up as a wrong number on someone's dashboard. Clean data upstream is the whole reason downstream work is trustworthy.

Your first day

Do these five things

  1. Open the base and find the hygiene view, that is admin home.
  2. Work it top to bottom, filling every yellow cell you find.
  3. Search for records with no Page, that is your orphan hunt.
  4. Clear one orphan by linking it to the right Page.
  5. Check the view is emptier than when you started.
