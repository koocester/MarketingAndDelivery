Part one

What Poppy AI is

A canvas, not a chat box.

In plain English
A visual workspace, not a linear chat.

Instead of one chat thread that scrolls forever, Poppy AI gives you an open canvas. You drag your sources onto it, arrange them however you think, and open an AI chat that can see everything on the board at once.

▸ YouTube videos & podcasts ▸ PDFs & documents ▸ Transcripts & voice notes ▸ Images & screenshots ▸ Website links

Part two

Getting started

The basic flow, then watch it live.

The basic flow
  1. Create a board. Open a fresh canvas for the topic, video or project you're working on.
  2. Add your sources. Drag in the video link, PDF, transcript or doc you want to work from.
  3. Chat across them. Open the AI chat on the board and prompt it — ask for a summary, the hooks, or a script draft.
  4. Pull the output. Take what the AI gives you into your storyboard, caption or script.
