Xero

Cloud accounting software — invoicing, bank reconciliation, bills, and the reports that tell the real story. This is where the books live.

What Xero is
Xero is for accounting management.

Invoicing, bank feeds and reconciliation, bills, and reports — including the P&L. If money moved in or out of the company, Xero is where it gets recorded and reconciled. It is not an expense-claims tool — that job belongs to Aspire.

What success looks like
You're winning when…
Every invoice goes out on timeno revenue sitting uninvoiced
The bank feed is reconciled dailynever left to pile up at month-end
Receivables get chasednothing unpaid gets forgotten
Reports match realitythe P&L reflects what actually happened

This is the bar. Everything that follows is how you hit it — and the final gate checks you against it.

Why finance uses Xero

One system for the money story.

  • Single source of truth — every invoice and bill lives in one place, not scattered across spreadsheets.
  • The bank feed connects directly to the accounts, so transactions aren't manually re-typed.
  • Reports roll up automatically — P&L and balance sheet update as the books are kept current.
  • Every transaction is auditable — who entered it, when it was reconciled, what it matched to.

How it fits

Xero is the books. The base is the work.

Xero = the books — invoicing, reconciliation, reports. It doesn't replace the Lark base workflow tracking, which is operational, not financial. Ties directly into the Finance reconciliation module: what gets reconciled daily in Xero is the raw material for every runway and cash conversation upstream.

Note   runway is only as real as the reconciliation behind it — a stale bank feed means a fabricated number.
