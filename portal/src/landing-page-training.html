What a landing page is
One page. One job.

A landing page has a single purpose: turn the visitor who tapped your offer into a captured lead. No menu, no detours, no other links. Everything on it either moves them toward the form, or it should not be there.

What a landing page wins you

Least friction, most database.

The spine is simple: reduce friction as much as possible, capture as much of the database as possible. It also wins by:

  • Qualifying — one or two smart questions filter for high-quality leads.
  • Speed-to-lead — instantly delivers the magnet and fires the follow-up while intent is hot.
  • Segmenting — captures just enough to route the lead to the right offer.
  • Retargeting — even non-converters become an audience you can chase.
  • Direct book or buy — for high-intent traffic, skip the email and go straight to a call.

The headline

One clear promise, the dream outcome.

The visitor decides in a second whether to stay. Lead with the outcome they want, not with your brand or your process. If they cannot tell what they get and why it matters from the headline alone, rewrite it. Clarity beats clever.

Proof

Make it believable.

A big promise raises a big doubt. Answer it with proof, real results, numbers, testimonials, or credibility markers. The more you claim, the more proof the page needs.

The ask

One action. The fewest fields.

Give the page exactly one call to action, repeated, with nothing competing for the click. Then ask for the minimum, usually a name and one contact. Every extra field costs you leads, so only ask for what you truly need now.

Message match
The page must match the video.

Whatever the video or carousel promised, the page must deliver the same words, the same offer, the same look. If the visitor feels they landed somewhere else, they leave. Keep the scent from hook to page unbroken.

Mobile first, fast

Built for a thumb, loads instantly.

Almost every visitor arrives on a phone, from a reel. Design for the thumb first, and keep it fast, a slow page loses leads before it even loads. Big tap targets, short copy, the form in reach.
