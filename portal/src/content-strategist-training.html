Lead the conversation through four moves.

1
SetTimeline · revision rounds · definition of done
2

3

4

Client objections

Turn the objection into a decision.

Your QC gate

Approve only when it's right.

The hook lands, the objective is served, it's brand safe and clean. When a fix is needed, you diagnose it and request a specific amendment. The Editor changes the cut, then you review the returned fix.

Getting started

Your first day

  1. Open the base and find your two home queues, Planning and Strategist QC.
  2. Open one video in Planning, read the idea, the objective and the page it serves.
  3. Map the storyboard, offer or lead magnet, landing page and shoot logistics. Follow the confirmed storyboard workflow once published.
  4. Set a shoot date and venue and see the calendar event build itself.
  5. Open one edit in QC, diagnose it fully, then practice Approve QC and Request amendment.
  6. Check the CS Updates channel, the base talks to you there.
