Part one

Qualify, then win

Not every lead deserves a slot in the pipeline.

Your mission

Bring in the right clients, not just any client.

Qualify before you invest time. A client who fits the brand and can be delivered well is worth ten who can't. Qualify hard up front and delivery never inherits a mismatch.

Your home

Clients and Vendor (Sales) is your table.

It holds the client, the deal, and the pipeline stage. Everything you own lives here. Keep it current and the pipeline reads itself.

Sales connects to delivery
Client→ Clients and Vendor (Sales)→ Project (Delivery)→ Every contracted Page→ Video + carousel deliverables

The Project groups the work. Sales selects every Page the client bought, so automation creates the right deliverables and assigns the delivery team. Sales owns commercial accuracy; CX owns client communication after the formal introduction.

What you fill

The base brief starts production. The full client handoff also requires Sales to introduce CX in the client group and brief CX, the Content Strategist and Producer before kickoff.

Client work has deadlines

Flag risk early.

A video for a client carries a due date. If anything looks at risk of missing it, raise it early. A client deadline missed quietly is the worst way to lose them.

Part two

The relay

Delivery receives the work. CX owns client communication after Sales completes the formal handoff.

Your mission

Write the brief once, write it right.

Name the client, deal, deadline, every contracted Page and everything promised. Delivery should start without guessing, and CX should lead the client without reconfirming what was sold.
