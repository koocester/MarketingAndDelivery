Build an offer in 5 moves
  1. Write the dream. What result do they badly want?
  2. List what's stopping them. Every problem in the way.
  3. Solve each one. One fix per problem.
  4. Pick the format. Guide, tool, template, session.
  5. Cut the weak, stack the rest. Into one offer.

Part two

The lead magnet

Alex Hormozi · $100M Leads

What a lead magnet is
A complete fix for one small problem.

The free thing a viewer gets in exchange for becoming a lead. A great one solves a single, specific problem completely and gives a quick win, good enough to sell, but given free so it pulls them toward the paid offer.

From: Alex Hormozi, $100M Leads
Your turn · build one in 7 steps

Pick a real page and fill each blank in your head as you go:

  1. The one narrow problem you'll solve → ______
  2. How you solve it → ______
  3. The format (checklist, guide, tool) → ______
  4. Its name → ______
  5. Make it easy to consume → how?
  6. Make it worth consuming → over-deliver how?
  7. The next step to the paid offer → ______
Worked example · the same 7 steps, filled in
1Problem — I don't know my home's value
2Solution — an expert valuation
3Format — a 1-page valuation report
4Name — "24-hour luxury home valuation"
5Easy — just send 3 photos
6Worth it — real comparables + a price range
7Next step — book a strategy call to sell
Write the call to action
Keep the call to action as short as you can.

A shorter call to action has less friction. Fewer words, and shorter words, make it easier to act. Same logic as questions: more of them adds friction, so fewer and shorter always wins.

Long & wordy
"Click here now to fill in the form below and book your free no-obligation consultation call with one of our expert advisors today"
High friction
Short & punchy
"Get my valuation"
Low friction · wins
What makes a lead magnet actually convert

It is the first rung

The free magnet is step one of a value ladder, it must pull the viewer up to the paid offer.

principle from Russell Brunson

The promise is clear

In one line the viewer knows exactly what they get and why it matters. Confusion kills conversion.

principle from Building a StoryBrand

Proof and reciprocity

Show it works, then over-deliver, so the viewer feels they owe you the next step.

principle from Cialdini's Influence
Where the offer lives

One lead magnet per video.

The lead magnet is set per video. When the producer marks a video carousel-worthy, that carousel inherits the same lead magnet. Every piece of content carries one offer, and every offer earns a lead.

Part three

When the client brings the offer

Don't just take it. Raise it.

Negotiate the offer up
A weak client offer wastes great content.

When a client hands you the offer, coach them to a stronger one. Run it through the same equation:

  1. Raise the dream — push for a bigger, more specific result.
  2. Raise the belief — add proof, a guarantee, a track record.
  3. Shrink time and effort — faster and easier to claim.
  4. Stack a bonus — one extra that costs little, adds a lot.
Walk-away: if the offer stays weak, flag it to the Head of Growth before we run it.
Handle the client's objections

Acknowledge, diagnose, reframe, soft close.

Objection: "I don't want to give away too much for free."

  1. Acknowledge — "Totally fair, you want to protect the value."
  2. Diagnose — "Is it the cost, or the worry it replaces the paid thing?"
  3. Reframe — a strong free offer earns trust and pulls buyers to the paid sale.
  4. Soft close — "If the paid offer stays clearly bigger, open to a stronger free one?"
