Part one

How we look

Colour, type, logo, imagery.

Colour

One maroon. No gold.

White
#FFFFFF · 60%
Black
#000000 · 30%
Maroon
#C02025 · 10%

Roughly 60% white, 30% black, 10% maroon. Maroon is a single accent, a button, a rule, a highlight, never a wash. There is no gold in this brand.

Colour pairings

Right pairings, wrong pairings.

✓Correct — use these

Black on white
White on black
White on maroon
Maroon on white

✗Incorrect — never

Maroon on black
Pale tint as text
Grey #999 as text
Maroon full wash

Pale tints and greys are for backgrounds and dividers only. Never set words in them. Notice the wrong column is hard to read — that is the point.

Type

Helvetica, done right.

✓Do this

Lead with the answer
One Helvetica hierarchy. Bold for emphasis. Sentence case, left aligned, generous white space.

✗Not this

Lead with the answer
ALL capitals, a second typeface, centred lines and cramped spacing.

One typeface, one hierarchy per view. Bold does the emphasis work, capitals never do. White space is a feature, not a gap to fill.

The logo

koocesterwealth

The wordmark is lowercase koocester in black, with the descriptor in maroon: group, homes, business, foodie, autos, wealth. In writing we capitalise "Koocester". The logo itself stays lowercase. Always use the official files in the brand folder. Never retype it.

Part two · this matters most

How we sound

The voice, and the words.

Your look gets attention. Your voice earns trust, and it is the part people remember. If you master one thing in this whole guide, master this.
The voice

Direct and brief, from abundance.

  • Lead with the answer. Bullets beat paragraphs.
  • Possibility framing. We open doors, never confuse abundance with flattery.
  • Truth over comfort. Say the accurate thing, with numbers.
  • Warm, not soft. Confident, not loud. Show, do not boast.
Question 1 · brand voice
A team member opens a client update with three sentences of pleasantries before getting to the result.
What's the on-voice fix?
✗
✗Non-negotiable · never cross this line

Negativity is not our voice.

A hard taboo, not a preference — the one line in this guide you never cross. It applies to everything you put out: every message, every reply, every caption, down to the smallest note or DM.

✗Never lead with the rejection words — no, don't, can't, won't, never, "that's a problem", "unfortunately". Negativity is not acceptable in anything we put out.
✓Always lead with the positive first — what works, what's possible, the way forward. Say the true thing from possibility, never from apology or fear.

The voice · inside the team too

It's how we talk to each other.

The voice isn't only for clients. Same move on a status update, a team message, a handover note — lead with the point, reframe the block into the way forward. A teammate reads it the way a client would.

"I'm blocked, the assets never came."→"Ready to ship the moment assets land — here's the one file I need to move today."
"This deadline is impossible."→"Two of these land on time today — here's what gets the last one there by Wednesday."
"Editing's behind again, sorry."→"Three of five are cut — here's the plan that clears the last two by noon."

Same honesty, opened from possibility. A blocker still gets named — with the way forward leading. Internal or external, one voice.

Voice · the core move

Positivity reframing.

Lead with the positive, reframe every negative into the way forward, and speak from abundance, not scarcity. This is the single move that most makes us sound like Koocester.

"We can't do that by Friday."→"We can have it right for you by Tuesday — here's how."
"That's a problem."→"Here's the fix, and it's quick."
"Your budget is too small."→"Here's what that budget can turn into — qualified buyers, on the numbers."
"No, we don't offer that."→"Here's what works even better for what you're after."

Same truth, opened from possibility. Numerals like 1–9 are fine — the words are what carry the voice.

What good looks like

You've got it when

  • Anything you make uses only white, black and one maroon accent.
  • You never retype the logo, you use the official file.
  • You lead with the answer, and reframe every "no".
  • A stranger could tell your work is Koocester without seeing the logo.
Brand voice · make it stick

Three voice moves, made distinct.

They work together, but they are not the same thing. Know which is which.

Move 1
Lead with the point
Answer first, context after. "38 qualified buyers — here's how we got there." Never bury the takeaway.
Move 2
Positivity reframing
Turn every "no / can't / problem" into the way forward. "Here's what works" — never the rejection words.
Move 3
Abundance, not scarcity
Speak from possibility, never fear. "Here's what becomes possible" — not "before it's too late."
1Take one thing you wrote last week and lead with the point — move the answer to the first line.
2Find every "no", "can't" or "problem" and reframe it into "here's what works".
3Read it back — does it sound like abundance, and is it white, black and one maroon?
