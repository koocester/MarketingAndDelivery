One-to-ones

A regular one-to-one to connect, grow, and support each other. Same flow every time — so nobody gets left behind and everybody keeps rising.

Why the one-to-one matters

This is how we stay connected, grow, and unblock each other.

A one-to-one is dedicated time to be honest, be heard, and move forward together. It's where progress gets seen, blockers get cleared, and people feel genuinely supported. Show up open — you'll both leave lighter and clearer.

Ground rule · one thing to do first
Always record the session.

Hit record in Lark Meeting at the very start — every time. Lark's built-in meeting recording keeps the conversation fully present (no scrambling to take notes), nothing meaningful is lost, and every action agreed is captured cleanly for follow-up.

Do it first: start the Lark Meeting recording before the conversation begins, so the whole session is captured from the opening word.
How we show up
  1. Keep it constructive — all sharing and feedback is grounded in mutual respect and given to help, not to score points.
  2. Keep an open mind — every conversation is a chance to improve together. Come curious, ready to learn something new.
  3. Respect the journey — honour each other's experiences, perspectives and growth. That respect is what builds trust and unity.
Before we start

The agenda

To start, set the shape of the session. Eleven stops, in order — from reflection to real life. Same flow every time, so it's always safe and easy to open up.

Agenda · step 1 of 11
1
What have you learned since we last spoke?
Which lessons, mindset shifts or meaningful takeaways stand out for you? Let's start by looking back at what's grown.
Agenda · step 2 of 11
2
What lit you up recently?
Which experiences or moments meant a lot or taught you a lot — and what did they give you?
Agenda · step 3 of 11
3
Where are you making progress, and where are you stuck?
What's moving, what's blocking you — and exactly how can I help you move it forward?
Agenda · step 4 of 11
4
How have you grown lately?
Which skills have you sharpened, which mindsets have evolved, and where do you feel more confident? Let's name it out loud.
Agenda · step 5 of 11
5
What feedback do we have for each other?
What constructive 360° feedback can we share to uplift and strengthen how we work together — offered warmly, received openly?
Agenda · step 6 of 11
6
How can we collaborate better?
Where could we improve synergy across the team and departments — so we work as one, not in silos?
Agenda · step 7 of 11
7
What's missing at the company level?
What could the company do better — and what's one realistic, do-able solution you'd suggest? Bring the problem and a first idea.
Agenda · step 8 of 11
8
What bold idea should we explore?
Think big — which creative strategy or growth hack could move the whole business forward?
Agenda · step 9 of 11
9
Where do you want your journey to go?
How can we align your role with your personal ambitions and growth — so the work carries you toward where you want to go?
Agenda · step 10 of 11
10
Is there anything outside work on your mind?
Any life challenge or decision where you'd welcome a perspective? We support the whole person, not just the role.
Agenda · step 11 of 11
11
Are we keeping this kind and growth-first?
How do we keep it kind, constructive and committed to growth — not ego? This is the spirit that holds the whole session together.
Before we end · the must-ask questions
Always close with these five.
What are you struggling with?
What pressure are you facing?
What kind of support do you need?
How can we help?
What are you grateful for?
