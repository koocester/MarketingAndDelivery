One role, two jobs

You sell, and you keep the base honest.

Sales brings the work in and sets it up to be delivered. Admin makes sure the data behind it stays clean enough for the whole base to run.

Part one

Sales

Win the client, then hand off clean work.

Your mission

Bring the clients in.

Win the client, then make sure their work is set up to be delivered. The sale is not done until delivery can pick it up cleanly.

Your home

Clients and Vendor (Sales) is your table.

It holds the client, the deal, and the pipeline stage. Everything you own lives here. Keep it current and the pipeline reads itself.

Sales connects to delivery
Client→ Clients and Vendor (Sales)→ Project (Delivery)→ Every contracted Page→ Deliverable shells

Sales owns selecting every Page the client bought on the delivery project. Your admin check is simple: every contracted Page is present, and each one produced the promised video and carousel shells.

What you fill
The client record The deal A clear brief

The brief is the base handoff. Write it so delivery knows exactly what was sold and can start without asking. The full client handoff also requires Sales to introduce CX and complete the internal pre-brief before kickoff.

Your numbers
Leading: deals in active pipeline this week.
Lagging: deals won this month.

Watch the leading number daily. It is the one you can still move. The lagging number is the result it produces.

Client work has deadlines

Flag risk early.

A video for a client carries a due date. If anything looks at risk of missing it, raise it early. A client deadline missed quietly is the worst way to lose them.

Part two

Keep the records true

The base only works if the data is clean.

Your mission

Keep the data clean.

Every automation, every dashboard, every assignment reads from these records. When the data is true, the base runs itself. When it slips, everything downstream slips with it.

The hygiene rule
Page Objective Assignee

Hygiene means no record you touch is missing these three. A bright-yellow cell means must fill, so fill it before you move on.

Watch for orphans

No Page means no team.

A record with no Page is an orphan: no team gets assigned, so nobody works it. A multi-Page client with one Page missing is also incomplete. Compare the signed handover against the project and clear every gap before a deadline runs out.

How assignment works
Born at Not Started→ Page linked→ Assigned automatically

Records are born at Not Started and take their team from the Page. Your job is to catch the ones that slipped through without a Page, because those never got assigned.

Where to look

The hygiene view.

It surfaces records missing key fields. Work it top to bottom until it is empty. An empty hygiene view is a base you can trust.

Your first day

Do these six things

  1. Open the base and find the Clients and Vendor table, that is sales home.
  2. Open one client and follow the chain, client to project to videos, see how work is traced.
  3. Create one practice client record and write a one-line brief.
  4. Admin: open the videos table and look for yellow cells and records with no Page.
  5. Anything without a Page has no team assigned, that is your catch.
  6. Flag one at-risk client deadline to the manager channel as practice.
