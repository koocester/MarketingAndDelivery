Your job on every shell · five fields

Five fields turn a shell into a brief.

  1. Fill the Video Idea: the hook and the angle for this post.
  2. Link the Lead Magnet: the free offer viewers get. Set it when the objective is Lead Generation.
  3. Set the Objective: exactly one, Lead Generation or Brand Awareness.
  4. Set the Success Target: define success with one measurable number.
  5. Set the Priority: show the team what must move first.
The Page and all dates are already set by the engine. These five are yours.
Every video · pick exactly one objective

Lead generation

the video earns the comment
  • A great hook — every video needs one
  • A keyword viewers comment to get the offer
  • A landing page + a ManyChat flow that replies automatically

Brand awareness

the video earns the view
  • A great hook — every video needs one
  • A story worth sharing
  • Views and reach, no hard offer
Success is measurable
If you can't measure it, it isn't a target.

Give every video one exact number: a lead target or a view target. The due date is already on the shell. Set the success target when you complete the brief, and performance becomes a fact the team can check.

Lead magnets
One lead magnet. Per video.

A lead magnet is the free offer viewers get for commenting. It attaches to the specific video, never to the Page — one video, one offer. If the objective is lead generation, set the lead magnet before you submit.

Part three

Approving the work

Videos and carousels. When to approve, when to reject.

Approving videos · you are the gate

You approve internal videos at Final Approval. Organic videos are approved by the Head Editor, client videos by the client, and carousels by the head copywriter — not you.

Approve →

  • Serves its objective (lead gen or awareness)
  • Hook stops the scroll in 3 seconds
  • Offer and call to action are set (lead magnet, ManyChat)
  • On-brand, and every yellow cell filled

Reject ✕

  • Misses the objective, however good it looks
  • Weak hook, or drags in the middle
  • No clear call to action, the lead leaks
  • Off-brand, or a required field empty

Reject with a specific reason, it drops to the fix-and-resubmit view, the clock starts there. No "approved but…".

These are the quick checks. For the full sweep and how to write amendments, do the Amending videos module.
Your part in carousels

One job: set the offer. Then hand it off.

A carousel is born from a worthy video, or straight from a project. It arrives on its Page and cannot move until its offer exists. Link the lead magnet in the Lead Magnets (Marketing) view. From there the copywriter writes it and the head copywriter approves it.

Part four

Overseeing

The team, and the projects.

Your accountability · non-negotiable

If it stalls, it's on you.

  • Own every project's deadline — on time, deliverables moving, no silent slippage.
  • Own the team's flow — nobody stuck, overloaded, or blowing a lead time on your watch.
  • Kill bottlenecks fast — the moment a record stops moving, you unblock it. Same day.
  • Guard the lead times — every role turns work around on time, every time.
  • Answer for performance — know what's winning, and feed it into next month.
  • Keep the calendar full — a thin pipeline is your problem before it's anyone else's.
The pipeline is yours end to end. Nothing stalling is ever "someone else's" — it's yours to catch, and yours to answer for.
