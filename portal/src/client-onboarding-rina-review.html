Review for Rina · client onboarding

One client.
One clear owner.

The proposed operating standard for the moment a deal is signed, the internal team is briefed and CX takes over the client relationship.

The operating decision

Sales hands over. CX owns from the introduction onward.

Sales · before and at signing

Own the promise and the transfer

  • Document signed scope, exclusions and commitments
  • Create the client group and add CX
  • Introduce CX as the single contact
  • Answer for commercial accuracy
CX · from the introduction

Own the client and the outcome

  • Accept the complete handoff
  • Own onboarding and communication
  • Schedule the internal pre-brief
  • Lead kickoff and the ongoing cadence
The handoff is complete only when the brief is documented, CX is introduced and the internal pre-brief is booked.

Internal pre-brief · minimum content

Leave aligned on five things.

1 · Client objective
What business result did they hire us to achieve?
2 · Scope and exclusions
What was sold, and what was not?
3 · Deliverables and timeline
What ships, on which Page and by when?
4 · Risks and dependencies
What access, assets or decisions could block delivery?
5 · Client questions
What must the kickoff confirm, and who will ask?
Output
CX can lead the kickoff without discovering scope live.
