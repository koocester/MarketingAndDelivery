Part two

Where things live

One record, one home. Learn which system owns it.

Where things live · your map of the company
Staff PortalFront door — Academy, dashboards, media and sales kits, reports and company tools. Start here when you need to find a system.
LarkOperating layer — chat, documents, files, calendar and approvals. Decisions belong in meeting notes; actions become assigned Lark tasks.
Marketing & Delivery BaseContent work — the source of truth for projects, deliverables, owners, due dates and stages. Update the record, not just the chat.
HubSpotSales records — leads, contacts, deals and sales activity. The sales rollout is still being completed; follow your Manager's current process until go-live is confirmed.
Xero · AspireFinance — Xero owns invoices and accounting; Aspire owns expenses, claims and company cards.
People/HR Base · Tech BasePeople and systems — the People/HR Base owns employee, role and department records; the Tech Base owns technology projects, systems, access and automation work.
Koocester AcademyProtocols and training — the approved way to perform recurring work, including each role's Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs).
Use the system that owns the record. Chat points people to the source of truth; it does not replace it.

How to ask well · three lines, every time

A good question answers itself halfway.

1
What you tried
Show you didn't outsource the thinking. "I checked Koocester Academy and tried X."
→
2
What you need
The exact thing you're stuck on, stated plainly — the specific gap, not just "help".
→
3
By when
The deadline the answer feeds — so they can prioritise you correctly.
Tried · Need · By when. Three lines turn a vague "I'm stuck" into a question anyone can answer in seconds.
