Part three

The monthly content engine

How every page fills itself with a month of work, on the 1st.

The single source of truth

The Posting Pattern sets the rhythm.

One config table decides, for every page, which days it posts, at what times, and how many slots it needs each month. Change the rhythm here and the whole engine follows.

PagePosting daysTimesSlots per month
Wealth · SGMon, Wed, Thu, Sun20:0012

This config table is the one place days, times and volumes are set. Everything downstream reads from it.

Every 1st of the month

The engine builds the month for you.

1st of the month→ Posting Pattern config→ Shells under the page-month harness

On the first of every month the system reads the config and auto-creates each page's video and carousel shells, with their intended upload dates and times already set. They land under a harness project for that page, that month.

Recurring Page content: the Page and month are already known, so the engine can build these shells without manual setup. Client projects use the Sales handover shown next.

Client work skips the queue

Client videos are born at Planning.

A normal video starts at Not Started. A client project video is created straight at Planning: it skips Not Started and Sourcing because the brief is already agreed. The base does this automatically the moment the client shell is made.

Not Started→ Sourcing→ Planning→ Ready to shoot→ …
Planning begins only after the brief is confirmed: Sales has handed over the signed promise, the internal team has aligned and CX has completed the client kickoff.

Part six

Data rules everyone holds

The few that keep the base clean.

Where the vertical lives

The page carries it.

The vertical and the country live on the page, the page being the common denominator that ties all the work together. A project just groups the work, and growth is measured page by month.

Hook & lead magnet
The hook and the lead magnet ensure performance.

Two things carry the numbers: the hook that stops the scroll, and the lead magnet, the free offer a viewer receives in exchange for becoming a lead. As a performance-marketing business, this pair is what turns views into pipeline. The lead magnet is set per video, and a carousel inherits it when the producer marks that video worthy.

Content strategist and the head of growth must be world-class at both. A strong hook with a strong lead magnet outperforms a great video every time.
The page does the work
The page assigns your team for you.

Producer, strategist, editor, copywriter and approvers all flow in automatically from the linked page. Adjust a name only for a genuine one-off, and the base keeps the rest in step.

Part seven

The two templates

Same pipeline, different intent.

Two templates, one difference: intent

Sensational

  • Built to be shared the instant it lands
  • Punchy hook, fast spread
  • The clip you send to a friend right away

Stories & Trust

  • Built to earn belief, credibility first
  • Fuller narrative, a story that lands
  • Stays with people, brings them closer

Both run the same length. Longer watch time builds trust, the more someone watches, the more they believe us, the way we binge YouTube.

Hook First frame Script tight Visual prompts Pacing Retention Captions Length B-roll Cut rhythm CTA Thumbnail
Why we plan every second

Every second has to earn its place.

Attention is scarce

Short attention spans and the algorithm both reward retention. Every second, every millisecond, decides whether someone stays or scrolls.

So we pack it tight

We plan the words to be as concise as possible and choose visual prompts deliberately, because a stronger frame holds attention longer.

So many variables ride on the plan, that is exactly why videos are planned before they are shot.
