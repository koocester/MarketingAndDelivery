The model in one line

A Project is only a harness. The real work lives on Videos and Carousels, each tied to a Page (which carries the vertical and country). Records are born blank at "Not Started" and move stage by stage, each move opened by a button and watched by a clock.

Three things to hold in your head: Pages carry vertical + country (not Projects). Lead magnets are per video. Growth is measured Page by month, never by project.

Who does what

The roles

Marketing coordinator

Plans the month, sets objective, page and offer. Fills the plan by the 15th.

Producer

Sources the guest, runs the shoot, uploads raw footage. Feeds the backlog.

Content strategist

Plans the storyboard, sets shoot date/venue, runs QC.

Video editor

Edits the cut, delivers the Frame.io link.

Copywriter

Writes the carousel: Canva design + caption.

Social media manager

Posts the finished piece, fills the post URLs and upload date.

Head of growth (approver)

The marketing gate: approves ideas/guests and final non-client sign-off.

Head copywriter (approver)

Final approval gate for carousels.

Manager

Watches the pipeline, clears bottlenecks, does not do stage work.

Most assignees default automatically from the Page. Override only for a one-off.

What the base does on its own

The automation layer

  • Fan-out. A new Project spawns its video and carousel shells automatically (guarded so it doesn't duplicate).
  • Auto-assign. New shells inherit Producer, Strategist, Editor, Copywriter and approvers from the linked Page's defaults.
  • SLA scans. Scheduled scans flag OVERDUE on editing, QC, amendments, raw upload, ManyChat and final approval, then nudge the owner.
  • Notifications. Role channels plus a direct message to the assignee when a record lands on them.
  • Calendar. "Add to calendar" creates the Lark shoot event; videos and carousels sync into the Content Calendar.
  • Must-fill colouring. An empty required cell turns bright yellow so nothing ships half-filled.
The non-negotiables

Data-model rules

  • Born blank. Every record starts at Not Started with no idea. The idea is added during planning, never injected at creation.
  • Pages carry the vertical and country, not the Project. A Project is just a harness that groups work.
  • Lead magnets are per video. A carousel inherits its offer from its source video.
  • Growth is measured Page by month, not by project.
  • Assignees default from the Page. Only override for a genuine one-off.

Why two flows feel different

The two templates

Sensational

Producer-originated. The producer generates the idea from a template and self-sources what to shoot. Used by Wealth and similar. Shells must be born blank, the producer adds the idea.

Stories & Trust

Guest / interview-led. Marketing and the strategist drive the concept and the guest. The planning flow fills the idea before approval.

This distinction is the reason the Wealth pre-seeding is wrong: Sensational content is the producer's to originate.
