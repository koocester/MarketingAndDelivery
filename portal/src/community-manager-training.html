Part one

The member journey

Six stages. Know where every member is.

One member · start to finish

Click through the member journey.

Discover and Join

The first hour decides the rest.

A new member is never more open than the moment they arrive. A warm, personal welcome inside the first day sets whether they lean in or drift off. So the welcome is not a nicety, it is the single highest leverage thing you do.

Onboard

One real interaction in week one.

A member who posts, replies or reacts in their first seven days stays. One who only lurks fades. So your onboarding has one job, get every new member to their first real interaction before the week is out.

Participate to Belong

Rituals turn visitors into regulars.

People come back for rhythm they can count on. A weekly prompt, a spotlight on a member, a recurring moment. Give them a role and a name in the room, and a visitor becomes someone who belongs.

Advocate

Belonging brings the next member.

A member who feels at home refers without being asked, if you make it easy. Give them a simple way to invite others and a reason to be proud, and your community grows itself while you route the warm intent to Sales.

Part two

Running the room

The daily craft.

The daily rhythm

Show up, spark, and lift others up.

  • Open the room every day. A quiet community dies quietly.
  • Start conversations, do not wait for them. Ask, prompt, provoke warmth.
  • Spotlight members, not the brand. People stay for people.
  • Reply fast. A message left unanswered teaches everyone to stop trying.
The Koocester voice

Lead with the point. Reframe every no.

Speak from abundance, never scarcity. Lead with the answer, then the warmth. When you cannot do something, never just say no, say here is what works instead. This is how Koocester sounds, and in the community you are that voice.

Part three

Events as activation

Where community becomes real.

Events

An event is a community moment.

The events programme is your best tool for belonging. Bring the community into every event, before, during and after. Capture who showed up, follow up while it is warm, and turn one night into lasting membership and real leads.

Part four

Tone and safety

Warmth needs boundaries.

Moderation · basic house rules

Warm room. Clear boundaries.

Everyone follows

  • Respect people; challenge ideas, not identities
  • Stay useful and relevant to the community
  • Protect member and company privacy

Promotion needs approval

  • No selling or self-promotion by default
  • Offers, links, recruitment and events need Community Manager approval before posting
  • Approved posts must be clearly labelled

Remove and escalate

  • Spam, scams and unsolicited promotional DMs
  • Abuse, unsafe advice or repeated rule-breaking
  • Anything legal, reputational, financial or client-sensitive → your manager
Simple rule: no selling in the community unless it is approved before posting.
Community servicing · own the resolution

Every member should know what happens next.

01Listen

Read the full context and the member's actual need.

02Acknowledge

Respond like a person; show the issue has been seen.

03Clarify

Ask only what is needed, then name the next owner.

04Resolve

Give the answer or keep the member updated while coordinating.

05Close

Confirm it is settled, record the learning and improve the room.

Escalate to your manager when the issue touches policy, money, safety, reputation, a client or another team's decision.
