How the score works

  • On-time delivery (35%): did your videos post by their intended date. The biggest factor, and fair to every role.
  • Consistency (20%): how many weeks you showed up, out of the weeks you could have. Steady beats spiky.
  • Output (15%): volume shipped, measured against your role.
  • Impact, leads (20%): qualified leads your videos captured, shared across the team who made them.
  • Impact, views (10%): reach, shared across the team who made the video.

Each video's impact is split across its makers: Producer 30%, Strategist 28%, Editor 22%, Social Media 20%. A hit rewards the whole team, not just whoever posted it.

Tiers

  • 💎 Diamond · elite reach or top points band
  • 🥇 Gold · strong and steady
  • 🥈 Silver · solid, room to climb
  • 🥉 Bronze · building momentum

Tiers reset each season so anyone can climb. The board rewards trajectory, not just history.
