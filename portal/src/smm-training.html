Part one

In the base

Where you look, and what you fill.

Your home view

The SMM Upload view is home.

SMM is you, the social media manager. Every video and carousel ready to post lands in this view. Rows still missing a post link are highlighted. Clear those first.

What you fill
Instagram Post URL TikTok Post URL Facebook Post URL Actual Upload Date

A post URL is the live link to what you just published. Post on each platform, paste each link into its field, then set the actual upload date, the day it really went live.

The button
Confirm all Uploaded

One press moves the record to Completed and flips the Posted flag. Only press it once the links are really in. It is your signature that the piece is live.

Post on time

On or before the date.

Every piece carries an intended upload date, the day the plan says it should go live. Post on or before it. A late post shows up in the manager brief.

Part two

Getting it right

The caption, the tags, the details that carry the brand.

The caption

Review it. Never post blind.

The caption field holds the text that sits under your post. An AI draft is already there. Read it, fix anything off, make it sound like us before you post.

Tag correctly

Use the handles you were given.

The producer captured the guest's Instagram and business handles. Tag those exactly. A wrong tag loses the reach that the whole shoot was built for.

One record, three fields

Before you post

  • Caption reviewed
  • Guest handles ready
  • Right accounts open

Everything staged first.

After you post

  • Every live link pasted
  • Actual upload date set
  • Confirm all Uploaded

Then it is truly done.

Part three

You and the numbers

Who reviews you, and how you are measured.

Who reviews you

The Head of Growth.

Mike watches your queue and your timing. Clear posts, real links and on-time dates are what he is looking for. Keep the board green and he never has to chase.

Your numbers
Leading: queue cleared each week.

Lagging: posts published on time this month. Clear the queue every week and the on-time number takes care of itself.

Your first day

Seven steps, start to done.

  1. Open the base and find the SMM Upload view. That queue is your day.
  2. The highlighted rows are missing post links. They are your priority.
  3. Open one ready video. Read the caption draft and fix anything off.
  4. Check the guest handles. They must be tagged.
  5. Post, paste every platform link, set the actual upload date.
  6. Press Confirm all Uploaded and watch it move to Completed.
  7. Check the SMM Updates channel daily.
Part four

The operating system

The rules that decide what posts, where, and when.

New: you own ManyChat now

ManyChat is your job.

ManyChat alert · SMM Updates channel→ You build the flow→ Press "ManyChat built"

ManyChat is the tool that turns a comment into a private message and delivers the offer. When the alert lands in the SMM Updates channel, you build the flow, then press the ManyChat built button on the record.

Changed: ManyChat used to be Tech's job. It moved to you, the social media manager. Do not wait on Tech, build it yourself.

One page, one day

Maximum one video per page each day.

A page never posts two videos at the same time. When a client video and an internal video land on the same day, use the intended upload time on each record and keep them at least 30 minutes apart.

Backlog · oldest first

The oldest ready content posts first.

At the start of each month, undated leftovers from last month take the first upload dates of the new month. Work the queue from the front.

The mix is already staged: per Page, 18 video shells = 8 Sensational + 10 Stories & Trust; 15 publish this month and 3 roll forward. Standalone carousels are 4 Sensational + 8 Trust, a 1:2 mix. Follow the dated queue.
