Management · Ownership

Accountability

The Command Dashboard tells you what is happening. This tells you who owns the problem and whether they closed it. A flag with no owner is a complaint. A flag with an owner and a date is a commitment.

The loop

Nothing counts until it reaches step four.
Step one

Flagged

Something is off. A missed lead time, a red metric, a broken promise to a client.

Step two

Owned

One name, not a team. If nobody owns it, it does not exist.

Step three

Actioned

A specific fix with a date on it, not an intention to look into it.

Step four

Closed

Evidence it is done. The owner closes it, and it is visible that they did.

The metric is loops closed, not loops raised. Raising flags is easy and makes a company look busy. Closing them is the only thing that changes a number. We measure closure rate and time to close, by owner.
