Aspire

Expense management — claims and reimbursements, company cards and spend. This is where the team gets paid back.

What Aspire is
Aspire is for expense management.

Claims and reimbursements, plus company cards and spend. It is not the accounting system — Xero holds the books, invoicing and reconciliation. Aspire is where staff get reimbursed and where card spend gets tracked.

What success looks like

This is the bar. Everything that follows is how you hit it — and the final gate checks you against it.

Why we use Aspire

Nobody should be out of pocket for long.

  • One system for claims and cards — staff submit in one place, finance reviews in one place.
  • A clear approval trail — every claim has a receipt, a reviewer, and a decision.
  • Keeps Xero clean — expenses get categorised and booked correctly, not dumped into the books as guesswork.
  • Fast turnaround — the sooner a claim is reviewed, the sooner someone gets paid back.

No receipt, no claim. No approval, no reimbursement. That order never changes — a claim without a receipt gets bounced back before it goes anywhere near approval.
Finance's part · reviewing a claim

Four checks, every time.

  1. Receipt attached — legible, matches the amount claimed.
  2. Category correct — filed under the right expense type, not "miscellaneous."
  3. Amount is reasonable — matches policy, no red flags.
  4. Approve or reject with a reason — a rejection without a reason just bounces back and forth.
Part two

Good to know

Practical habits, and one open item.

Keep in mind
  • Submit claims promptly — don't let a month of receipts pile up before filing.
  • Use the correct category — it keeps Xero's books clean downstream.
  • Receipts must be legible — a blurry photo slows down approval, not speeds it up.
  • Company cards still need records — card spend isn't exempt from the same discipline as a claim.
Confirm   our exact Aspire setup in-app — approval routing, categories and card policy can vary by workspace configuration, so check the live app rather than assuming.
