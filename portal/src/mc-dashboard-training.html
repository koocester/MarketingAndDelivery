The key idea

Lead numbers you can act on today. Lag numbers are the result.

Lead indicators

act on these today
  • Videos in pipeline
  • Shoots scheduled
  • Lead-gen posts live

Lag indicators

the result, already happened
  • Leads captured
  • Customers won
Watch the lead numbers to change the lag numbers. By the time a lag number moves, it's too late to fix this week — the lead number is where you still have a lever.

Reading, not looking

For every tile, ask three questions.

  1. Lead or lag? — decide what you can still act on today.
  2. Up or down vs target? — a number with no target isn't a target.
  3. What's the ONE action it tells me to take? — reading ends in a decision.
If a number doesn't change what you do next, you haven't finished reading it — you've just looked at it.
Reading ends in a decision
Double down on what's winning. Diagnose what's stalling.

Winning number → feed it into next month's ideas. Stalling number → diagnose with the funnel, same one you use on a single video.

Reach→ Watch→ Act→ Capture→ Qualify
A stalling dashboard number is a stalling video, scaled up. A dashboard you don't act on is wallpaper.
Build the habit

Read the drivers daily. Read the OMTM weekly.

OMTM · One Metric That Matters
Lead → Customer conversion rateThe single outcome that shows whether the growth system is turning captured leads into paying customers.
Customers ÷ Leads × 1004 customers ÷ 95 leads = 4.2%

Daily

2 minutes
  • Check the drivers: pipeline, posts live and lead capture
  • Find what is stalling before it reaches the OMTM
  • Take one action today

Weekly

15 minutes
  • Is Lead → Customer conversion up, flat or down?
  • Check both sides: lead volume and customers won
  • Feed what converts into next month's plan
Beyond the week · the quarterly loop

Every quarter, the posting days recalibrate.

Run the monthly schedule→ Measure on the Posting-Day Scorecard→ Recalibrate the Posting Pattern config↻
The loop. Every quarter the Posting Pattern (Config) table is recalibrated from the Metabase Posting-Day Scorecard, which shows which posting days perform for each page. Read the Scorecard, then update the config so the next quarter posts on the best days.
Where the data comes from
Explorer slots feed the Scorecard.

Proven days

most of the month's slots
  • Post on the days the Scorecard already ranks best

Explorer slots

1 to 2 per page per month
  • Test a new day
  • Send fresh data to the Scorecard
No explorers, no learning. Keep the test slots running every month and the quarterly recalibration keeps finding better days. Skip them and the config goes stale.
