The CRM, in plain English
One place. Every contact. Every deal.

HubSpot CRM is where Sales keeps track of the people you're talking to (contacts) and the businesses they work for (companies), the opportunities you're chasing as they move through a pipeline, what you need to do next (tasks), and the full history of every call, email and note tied to them.

Why Sales uses it

It's not admin. It's how you don't drop a deal.

  • Never lose a lead — everything's logged, nothing lives in your head or a notepad.
  • See the whole pipeline at a glance — who's where, what's stuck, what's about to close.
  • Always know your next action — a task tells you exactly what to do and when.
  • Seamless team continuity — anyone can see the full history and continue the work confidently.
The five things you'll touch every day

The core objects

  1. Contacts — a person: name, email, phone, job title. Every call, email and note attaches here.
  2. Companies — the business a contact works for. One company can hold many contacts.
  3. Deals — a real opportunity, moving through defined stages toward a close.
  4. Pipeline stages — the steps a deal moves through, e.g. appointment scheduled → qualified → proposal sent → closed won/lost.
  5. Tasks — the to-dos and reminders tied to a contact or deal, so nothing gets forgotten.
